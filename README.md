## 🚀 Get started

1. Download and install the APK above. Android may ask you to allow installation from your browser.
2. Open the app and choose a photo or video.
3. Pick a **Quality** or **Balanced** profile and tap **Start upscale**.
4. Compare the result and save it to your Gallery.

All eight models are included, so there’s no separate model download.

## 🎨 Pick your profile

| Profile | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Quality** | When image quality matters most |
| **Balanced** | A balance between quality and speed |
| **Speed · Beta** | Experimental faster processing |
| **IRL Quality · Beta** | Real-world photos and footage |

There are sharp and soft anime options, too.

> 🧪 **Speed models are in beta and may cause color changes. They aren’t recommended; start with Quality or Balanced instead.**

## 📱 Phones and performance

Designed for **Snapdragon 8 Gen 1 and newer**. Requires **Android 8.0+** and a **64-bit ARM phone**.

**Reported video upscaling speed: 6–25 FPS on Snapdragon 8 Gen 3**, with the higher speeds reached using Speed profiles. Your speed will depend on the profile, video resolution and phone.

Other ARM64 phones can try the GPU compatibility path. They need a supported OpenCL or Vulkan driver; there’s no CPU fallback, so compatibility varies.
